    ❓ How does grow-with-me sizing work?⌄

    Coats and dungarees have cuffs, hems and straps that lengthen by about two sizes. Pick the size for their height today (or one up if you want room) and the adjustments take care of the next year or so.

    ❓ Are the coats really waterproof?⌄

    Seams are taped and the fabric is coated, with an 8,000 mm rating in our (fictional) lab. For a day of full-on puddle stomping, that's plenty. Boots should be dried upside down on the stairs, as per tradition.

    ❓ Can I wash them?⌄

    Yes. 40 degrees, no fabric softener (it clogs the coating), and low tumble or line dry. Re-proof the coat once a year with a wash-in proofer.

    ❓ What if the size is wrong?⌄

    Swap it free, any time in 60 days, even if it's been worn to the park. Use the prepaid label in the parcel or ask us for a new one.

    ❓ Do you ship outside the country?⌄

    Not yet. This is a demo shop, so nothing ships at all, but a real Puddle & Pip would start with domestic delivery and add Europe next.
