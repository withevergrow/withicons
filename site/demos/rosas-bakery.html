      Est. 1961

      Three generations, one very old oven

      In March 1961 Rosa Valdivia signed a lease on a corner shop with a cracked window and a second-hand deck oven, and borrowed the deposit from her brother Tomás. She was twenty-six. She baked forty loaves that first morning and sold thirty-one. The rest went to the neighbours who had come to see whether the new baker would last.

      She did. Sixty-five years later the oven is still the same oven, the starter is still Old Nell, and the bread is still ready before the streetlights go off.

      A loaf is only done when the whole street smells like somebody's home.Rosa Valdivia, to every new apprentice

      Good questions

      Before you ask

      🚚 Do you deliver?⌄

      No. Rosa's is pick-up only, which is how the bread gets to you still warm. The one exception is wedding cakes, which we drive, carry in and set up within twenty-five miles.

      📅 How far ahead can I order?⌄

      Bread and pastries can be ordered up to ten days ahead, or for the same day while slots last. Order by 5 pm for tomorrow. Custom cakes need 48 hours, wedding cakes about eight weeks.

      ✏️ Can I change or cancel an order?⌄

      Yes, by phone until 5 pm the day before, no charge. Custom cakes can be changed until 24 hours out. We would rather hear early than throw out forty baguettes.

      💳 Do you take cards?⌄

      Cash and cards, both at the counter. Nothing is charged when you order ahead, so a change of plan costs you nothing.

      🌱 Do you bake gluten-free?⌄

      Not really. The almond-orange tea cake uses ground almonds instead of flour, but it is baked in a room full of wheat, so we cannot call it safe for coeliac customers. We would rather say so than guess.

      🐾 Can I bring my dog?⌄

      Dogs wait on the bench outside, and any dog that sits politely gets a plain biscuit from the jar by the door. Assistance dogs are welcome inside, always.
