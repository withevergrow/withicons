I · The Dress Code

Come as someone else

✨

Masquerade, black tie or beyond. The Great Hall has stood since 1887 and has never once forgiven polyester.

🎭

A mask, always

Cover at least your eyes and nose. It stays on until the unmasking at midnight. A basket of spares waits at the Gatehouse, along with a mirror and no judgement.

🎨

Four colours of the house

Black, bone, tarnished gold and blood red. A little ember orange will not be turned away.

BlackBoneGoldBlood
👔

Formal, in the old way

Tails, waistcoats, long gowns, lace gloves, Victorian mourning. The garden is cold, so bring a cloak, not a hoodie.

🚫

Please leave at home

Real or realistic weapons, full-face latex you cannot drink through, and glitter (it never leaves the house).

Not sure who to be?

Choose a persona and we will add it to your reply card. Nobody else will know which one you picked until the parade at eleven.

⏳

III · The Order of the Evening

Eight o’clock until the carriages

🔔

Doors at eight, midnight at twelve, carriages at one. Everything between is candlelit, and nearly all of it is free.

  3. 9:00 pm

VI · The Articles of the House

House rules

Seven articles, none of them negotiable, every one of them written after something went wrong.

🔪

Article I

No real blades

Props must be foam, cardboard or blunt. A cane is welcome; a sabre will be politely taken to the Gatehouse for safekeeping, and returned at one.

📵

Article II

No photos in the Séance Room

Phones sleep in velvet pouches at the door. Everywhere else, shoot freely, but ask before you tag a masked stranger.

🎭

Article III

Masks stay on until midnight

Not on your forehead, not in your hand, not “just for a second”. The footmen have been told to remind you, and they enjoy it.

🕯️

Article IV

The flames are real

Four hundred candles and a wooden staircase. Tie back long hair, gather loose sleeves, and keep a hand on your cape near the stairs.

🤝

Article V

Be good company

Ask before you touch, take no for an answer, and tell Theo or any footman if something feels wrong. We will walk you out.

🔑

Article VI

Drink slowly, leave sober

Hand your car keys to the Gatehouse if you want a drink. Keys are returned only to the sober, with a glass of water and a look.

🐈

Article VII

Respect the house cat

Marrow is twelve, black and in charge. Do not feed him, do not argue with him, and do not sit in his chair, which is every chair.

IX · Questions, Answered

Before you come

❓

The practical things, in the order guests ask them.

🅿️Where do I park?

In the Lower Meadow: sixty spaces, lit by lanterns, a four-minute walk uphill to the Gatehouse. Blue-badge and drop-off parking use the carriage drive. Tell the Gatehouse on 555-0119 and a footman will guide you in.

🧥Is there a coat check?

Yes, at the Gatehouse, free of charge. Coats, cloaks, capes, umbrellas and any prop you cannot dance with. You will receive a numbered raven-feather token; please do not lose it, as the ravens keep records.

🚕How do I get home safely?

Book one of the three sober-ride numbers in the directions above, or take the free Night Owl Shuttle from the Gatehouse at 12:30, 1:00 or 1:30. If you drove and drank, hand over your keys at the Gatehouse and collect them in the morning. Six guest rooms are available for those who would rather stay.

👥Can I bring a guest?

Yes. Tick the +1 box on your reply card and give us their name. Both of you must be 18 or over, and masked. We are sorry, but there are no walk-ins at the door: the candles are counted.

🍽️What about dietary needs?

Tell us on the reply card. The kitchen cooks vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and nut-free plates in advance, and the drinks list marks everything that contains alcohol.

🪪Is there an age limit?

18 and over, with photo ID if you look suspiciously young. Every Vance since 1887 hangs in the hall in oils, and none of them care for children at parties after dark.

♿Is the house accessible?

Step-free entry is by the carriage drive and a ramp into the Great Hall. The Library is a lit quiet room with chairs and no music. Tell Mara about mobility, sensory or seating needs on the reply card and she will arrange them without fuss.

🌧️What if the weather turns?

The house is the party, so only the embers in the garden depend on the weather. If it rains, the braziers move under the loggia and the cider moves with them. Dress for fog either way.

📷May I take photographs?

Everywhere except the Séance Room. Please keep masked guests’ faces out of anything you post until after the unmasking, and ask before you tag anyone.
