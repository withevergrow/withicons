The Tony's shave

Forty minutes you cannot rush.

We have done it the same way since 1974, and the only thing that changed is Dom's playlist. Four steps, no shortcuts.

  1. Hot towel

    A towel steamed to about 130 degrees goes over your face for a full minute and a half. Pores open, whiskers soften, your shoulders finally drop.

    🌡️ 130°F, wrung out by hand🧖

  2. Warm lather

    Shaving soap whipped in a brass bowl and brushed on with a boar-bristle brush, against the grain first so every hair stands up for the blade.

    🖌️

  3. Straight razor

    Two passes, with the grain and then across it, on a blade stropped that morning. Nothing electric, nothing rushed, nothing you will regret.

    🪒

  4. Cold towel and balm

    A cold towel to close things up, then bay rum splash and a little unscented balm. You walk out smelling like a barbershop, which is the point.

    ❄️

Since 1974

Same corner. Same pole. Three chairs.

Antonio “Tony” Russo opened the shop with nine hundred dollars, two chairs and a pole on loan from his cousin. Fifty years on, his son Dom cuts hair in the same room, the radio still plays the same station, and half the customers are the grandsons of the first ones.

  1. 1974🏪 The doors openA former shoe-repair shop at 214 Alder Street, two chairs, one mirror that does not quite hang straight.
  2. 1986👥 The third chairBought at an estate sale two towns over. It is still Chair 3, and it still creaks on the left.
  3. 1998✂️ Dom takes his licenceTwenty-two years old, three chairs, and a father who watched every single cut.
  4. 2009🔑 The keys change handsTony retires on his seventieth birthday. He still comes in on Thursdays for coffee and opinions.
  5. 2024🏅 Fifty yearsFifty years on Alder Street. The pole has been rewired once, which Dom considers a lot.

Before you come in

Questions we get at the counter.

📅 Do I need an appointment?⌄

No. Walk-ins are always welcome, and the walk-in board at the top of this page shows the current wait. An appointment holds a chair for you, which is worth it on Fridays and Saturdays.

🕒 How long does a haircut take?⌄

A classic cut is about 30 minutes, a skin fade 40, and a hot-towel shave 40. The Full Tony (cut and shave together) is 70 minutes, and we do not watch the clock during it.

👶 Do you cut kids' hair?⌄

Every day. Kids 12 and under are $18, and that includes a booster seat, a cape with dinosaurs on it, and a lollipop. For a first haircut, ask for Ines, and bring a favourite toy.

💳 Do you take cards?⌄

Yes, cards and tap. Cash is appreciated, and tips in cash go straight to the barber who cut your hair.

🗓️ What if I need to cancel?⌄

Change or cancel up to two hours before and it costs nothing. After that, text the shop and we will sort it out. We do not charge no-show fees, we just remember.

🎁 Do gift certificates expire?⌄

Never. They work for any service and for anything on the shelf, and the balance stays on the card until it is used up.

🖼️ Can I bring a photo of the cut I want?⌄

Please do. A photo on your phone is the fastest way to avoid a conversation that begins with “a little off the sides”. Marcus especially loves a reference.
