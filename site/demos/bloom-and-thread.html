04 Our story

Two friends, one sewing table above the old hardware store.

Bloom & Thread began in 2016 on Imogen Hale’s kitchen table, with a borrowed sewing machine and a stack of vintage dress patterns. Priya Raman, a weaver who lived upstairs, came down for a cup of tea and stayed for the next ten years.

Today we are eleven makers working from the old hardware store on Cobbler’s Row. We cut each piece in a run of forty or fewer, finish the edges by hand and sell what we make through this little shop and one slightly larger one on the market square. Nothing is made until there is room for it on the rail.

💗 Imogen & Priya

Shipping, returns, payment and care

🚚Shipping

Free over $75, otherwise a flat $6.50. Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day, with tracked delivery in 2 to 4 days across the US.

Changed your mind? Return anything unworn within 30 days. Exchanges are free, and refunds land within 5 days of your parcel arriving.

Pay with any major card or digital wallet, or use a Bloom e-gift card. Orders over $100 can be split into four interest-free payments.

Wash cool, dry flat, press while damp. Every piece comes with a mending-thread card in matching colours, because we want you to keep it.
