🤝 Our story

It started on a living-room floor.

In 2011 Nell and Ravi Hartley spent a rainy month searching for one wooden train that would not fall apart in their son's hands. They never found it, so they opened a shop instead.

Today Little Sprout is still the two of them, a handful of neighbors who know every toy by name, and a test panel of four sticky-fingered children. We only stock what survives them.

ℹ️ Good to know

Questions parents ask

🍼 How do I pick the right age?▾
Every toy shows the age we recommend, based on age grading and our test panel. If your child sits between two bands, go up for sturdy toys like blocks and down for anything with small parts. The age finder above does the sorting for you.
🛡️ Is every toy really tested?▾
In this fictional demo, the sample copy says every toy is checked against EN 71 and ASTM F963 by an independent lab before it reaches the shelf. A real shop would publish the report for each toy right here.
🚚 How much is delivery?▾
Free over $50, otherwise a flat $5.95. Orders placed before 3 pm ship the same day, packed in paper and cardboard.
🎁 Can you gift-wrap?▾
Yes. Switch on gift-wrap in your basket: recycled paper, a cotton ribbon and a handwritten card with your message. It is $4, and free on orders over $75.
🔨 What if a toy breaks?▾
Wooden toys are repaired free for life. Send us a photo and we will mail a fix kit or a spare part. If it cannot be fixed, we will replace it.
↩️ What is your returns policy?▾
Thirty days, unused and in the original packaging. We include a free return label in every parcel.
