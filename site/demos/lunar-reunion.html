The practical bits

Everything you need to know

Reunion dinner is the most important meal of the year: everyone comes home, everyone eats too much, nobody leaves empty-handed.

Sat 6

Arrive by 6:00 pm

The lion dance greets every guest at the gate at 6:15. Come early for tea, tangerines and a family photo under the lanterns.

👕

Wear red

Red keeps bad luck away. Gold and jade are welcome too. Please skip all-black and all-white, which are for mourning.

🎁

Bring in pairs

Fruit, tea or sweets, always two of everything. No clocks, no pears, no sharp gifts. Red envelopes for the little ones, please.

📍
18 Plum Blossom CourtRed gate with two paper lanterns. Street parking on Lantern Road, or the free lot behind the temple.

Getting here

  • 🚗By car: 7 minutes from the ring road. Free parking after 5 pm on Lantern Road.
  • 🚆By train: Jade Canal station, then a 6 minute walk past the tea house.
  • 📞Lost? Call Daniel on 555-0142 and he will wave from the gate.
  • 👶Little ones welcome: high chairs, a kids' table, and a quiet room upstairs.

Hongbao etiquette

How to give a red envelope

A few small rules keep the luck flowing in the right direction.

  1. 1

    Use crisp new notes

    Fresh bills, never torn or folded. Even amounts only; avoid anything with a 4.

  2. 2

    Two hands, a small bow

    Offer with both hands and a warm blessing: gong xi fa cai, or simply happy new year.

  3. 3

    Receive with both hands

    Say thank you and a blessing back. Keep it closed until you are alone.

  4. 4

    Married guests give

    Unmarried guests, children and our favourite students receive. Pass it on later.

Pick an envelope

Choose one of three. Inside is this year's blessing for you (the money is imaginary, the wishes are real).

Good questions

Frequently asked

👥Can I bring a friend or a plus one?▾

Of course. Just add them to your headcount in the RSVP. A reunion dinner is better with strays.

🥬Is there food for vegetarians and allergies?▾

Yes. Tell us in the form and Grandma Mei makes a vegetarian version of the dumplings and noodles. Menu cards on the tables list the common allergens.

👶Are children welcome?▾

Very. There is a kids' table, a quiet room upstairs, and every child gets a red envelope from the hosts.

🧧How much should I put in a red envelope?▾

Whatever feels warm and comfortable, in an even amount and never a 4. Gifts for the hosts are not expected; your company is the gift.

🚗Where do I park?▾

Street parking on Lantern Road is free after 5 pm, and the temple lot behind the house has 20 spaces. Please carpool; the fireworks traffic is real.

🧨What happens if it rains?▾

Rain is lucky. The lion dance moves into the courtyard and the fireworks slide to 10:30 pm or the following night if it is stormy. We will text everyone by 4 pm.

Gong xi fa cai

Thank you for travelling home with us. We hope the year ahead brings you more luck, more family, and at least one more helping of noodles.
