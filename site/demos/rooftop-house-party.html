            House rules

            Drafted by the neighbours, edited by us.

            Seven other flats live in this building and two of them have babies. Be the guest they would invite back.

            • 🌙

              Quiet after 11

              Speakers go off at 11 sharp and the last hour is a silent disco on headphones. Keep it low in the stairwell, too: it carries.

            • ⬆️

              Stairs only

              Five flights to the roof and no elevator on that route. Hold the rail, take your time, and tell Dee if stairs are a problem.

            • 🚫

              Nothing over the ledge

              Stay behind the low wall. No glass within six feet of the edge: cans and the plastic cups in the bin only.

            • 🔥

              No flames, no smoking

              The string lights are LED and the roof is tar. Smokers have the Pike Street side door, two minutes away.

            • ♻️

              Leave it nicer

              Blue bin for cans and bottles, black for everything else. There are bags by the roof door and a cleanup crew at midnight.

            • 🚪

              Do not prop the street door

              Buzz and wait. If the door is open when you arrive, close it behind you. Everyone who lives here has a key.

            Buzz 4C. Then climb.

            Questions

            Everything people text Dee about.

            Still stuck? Call or text 555-0142 and she will answer between the grocery run and the stairs.

            ☂️What if it rains?⌄

            Then it becomes an indoor party in apt 4C and the fourth-floor hallway, with the same sets and the same tacos. We will message the Roof Crew chat by 4 pm on Saturday, and we only call it for rain or lightning. Right now the chance is 5%.

            ➕Can I bring a plus-one?⌄

            Yes, one, please. Add them on the RSVP form so we can count blankets and tacos. A bigger group? Message Dee first: the roof tops out at 40 people.

            🛡️Kids? Dogs?⌄

            Adults only, sorry: the ledge is low and the night runs late. Calm dogs are welcome before 9 pm if you tell us in advance, but the stairs are long and the gravel is hard on paws.

            ⬆️Is the roof accessible?⌄

            Honestly, not very: it is five flights of stairs and no elevator to the roof. If that is a problem, tell Dee. We will put a seat by the window in 4C, which has an almost-as-good skyline view, and a speaker on the same set list.

            🚕How do I get home?⌄

            The last bus (14) leaves Foundry & 3rd at 12:40 am and the last Mercer Street train is at 1:20. Rideshares pick up on Pike Street, which is quieter than the front door. Please do not drive if you have been drinking; the couch in 4C is real.

            🍕Is it free? What about the pizza?⌄

            It is free. Please bring a drink and claim something on the bring-sheet. At 9 pm two people collect four pizzas from the place on Pike; we split the bill, about $8 a head if you are eating.

            🎧What is the headphone hour?⌄

            A silent disco. At 11 the speakers go off and 24 wireless headphones come out, on three channels you can switch between: pink for house, violet for pop, orange for whatever Marcus feels like. First come, first headphoned; the rest can dance to the quiet.
