    Questions

    Are the loaders free?

    Yes. Like every icon on with icons they are free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license. No sign-up, no credit needed.

    Do they need JavaScript?

    No. Every loader is one SVG file that animates itself with a few lines of CSS inside it, so <img src="…/spinner.svg"> just works. The optional <with-loader> tag adds colour, speed, morph sequences and a finished state.

    Will they slow my page down?

    They animate only transform and opacity, which the browser runs on the GPU, off the main thread. A line loader is one to three kilobytes.

    What about people who prefer less motion?

    With reduced motion turned on, every loader slows to a calm pace instead of stopping, because a frozen loader looks like a hang. Each one is announced as "Loading" (or your words) to screen readers.

    Can a loader turn into a check mark when the work is done?

    Yes: call done() on the <with-loader> element and it blooms into a check (or any icon you name). reset() starts it again.
