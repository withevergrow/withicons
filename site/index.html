        What is with icons?
        with icons is a free icon library by Evergrow. It has 734 icons, each drawn in 34 styles, plus live icons for dates, counts and labels (over 25,000 in all), and every icon comes with its own animation. Copy them into slides, docs and design tools, download them in 36 file formats, install them for React, Vue and more, or let your AI agent add them. Everything is free under the MIT License.
        
          
        Are the icons really free?
        Yes. All 734 icons in all 34 styles, animations included, are free for personal and commercial work under the MIT License. No sign-up, no payment, and you don't have to credit us (though we love it when you do).
        
          
        How do I put an icon in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Canva?
        Find an icon, pick a style and colour, then press Copy image and paste it in. Or download a PNG or SVG and insert it like any picture. In PowerPoint and Keynote, SVG stays sharp at any size and you can recolour it. Our guides walk through each app step by step.
        
          
        Are the icons animated?
        Yes. Every icon comes with its own animation that suits it: a bell rings, a heart beats, a loader spins. It works in all 34 styles. Play it on a loop or on hover, or let one icon turn into another, like play into pause. No code needed: download a GIF for PowerPoint or Google Slides, an MP4, an animated SVG or a Lottie file. On websites, the optional @withicons/motion add-on plays them with a stylesheet and a class, and keeps them still for anyone who turns on reduce motion. Try it above.
        
          
        What are live icons?
        Icons whose content you set: a calendar with your date, a clock with your time, a bell with your count, a battery at your level, a tag with your words. There are 50 of them, in all 34 styles. Download one for slides and docs, or add one line of HTML and a calendar shows today's date on your website. See all live icons.
        
          
        What's the difference between the styles?
        Every icon is drawn once and comes in 34 looks, in six groups. Essentials, for apps, websites and documents: Line (clean outlines), Solid (bold filled), Duo (outline with a soft tint and an accent colour) and Suite (enterprise colour icons). Product & brand, for SaaS sites, launches and bold brand graphics: Bento (icons in tinted tiles), Dock (app-icon tiles), Brutal (bold neo-brutalism) and Bauhaus (primary colours and pure geometry). 3D & glass, for hero sections and decks: Clay (soft 3D clay), Glass (soft frosted glass), Liquid (clear refractive glass), Chrome (liquid metal), Soft 3D (soft studio-lit 3D), Luxe (premium layered 3D) and Skeuo (real materials and depth). Playful, for kids, social posts and games: Kawaii (cute, with a tiny face), Plush (soft felt toys), Sticker (die-cut Y2K sticker), Gloss (puffy and shiny), Pastel (soft candy pastels), Pixel (pixel art) and Retro (70s sunset stripes). Artistic, for print and themed designs: Sketch (hand-drawn marker), Engrave (banknote-style lines), Blueprint (technical drawing), Anime (cel-shaded anime art), Gothic (cathedral stone and stained glass) and Coquette (bows, pearls and blush pink). Holidays, for festivals and seasonal campaigns: Utsav (Indian festive craft), Rangoli (Diwali, Durga Puja and Holi), Halloween (spooky-cute), Christmas (cosy, with snow), Lunar New Year (lucky red and gold) and Valentine (cute hearts and blush). Not sure? Line is the safe choice for most work, or tell us what you're making.
        
          
        Can I change the colour and size?
        Yes. Pick any colour before you download, or recolour an SVG later in your design tool. The colourful styles also come with more than 20 ready-made palettes for every icon. SVGs stay perfectly sharp at every size; PNGs come in sizes from 64 to 1024 pixels.
        
          
        Do I need to credit with icons?
        No credit is required. The MIT License only asks that the license text travels with the source code if you redistribute the icon files themselves as a library. Read the license in plain English.
        
          
        I'm a developer. Is there an npm package?
        Yes. Packages for React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, Solid, web components and CSS icon classes are on npm under @withicons, for example npm i @withicons/react. They are typed, tree-shakable and have zero dependencies. Prefer no install? Use the CDN, copy SVG code or download files from any icon page. See Developers.
        
          
        Can AI agents use these icons?
        Yes. Run npx withicons init to add the agent skill and the MCP server to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other AI tools, or connect the remote server at https://withicons.com/mcp. Your agent then searches every icon by meaning, adds the right one to your code and can export files. There is also llms.txt for any AI that reads the web. See For AI.
